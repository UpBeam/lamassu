# ADR 0002: UpBeam Research project attribution

Date: 2026-10-24
Status: accepted

## Context

The project needs a consistent public identity as its design and implementation evolve. The project owner has proposed UpBeam Research, a subdivision of UpBeam OY, with research.upbeam.net as its research address.

## Decision

Attribute development to UpBeam Research in the README, research direction, stewardship document, and package metadata. Retain Lamassu as the working project name, the existing GitHub repository, the MIT license, and existing copyright notices.

## Consequences

Research attribution is consistent across the repository. Website deployment and repository transfer remain separate operations. The public presentation must continue to distinguish the research ambition from the small implemented prototype. No new corporate history, personnel, certifications, or service commitments are implied.
